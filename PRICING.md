# Commercial Pricing

This project is designed for a clean dual-license model:

- Open-source / non-commercial use: MIT license
- Commercial use: separate commercial license agreement

## Recommended pricing tiers

### 1. Evaluation / prototype
- Price: $0
- Use: internal evaluation, research, non-commercial testing
- Includes: source access, docs, community support

### 2. Small business license
- Price: $499 / year
- Use: small commercial deployment, internal business use, product evaluation
- Includes: commercial-use rights, support window, licensing certificate

### 3. Growth license
- Price: $1,999 / year
- Use: SaaS, embedded product integration, commercial deployment at scale
- Includes: commercial-use rights, support and maintenance, customization review

### 4. Enterprise license
- Price: custom quote
- Use: multi-team or multi-product use, bespoke integration, white-label deployment
- Includes: enterprise terms, source review options, SLA-based support

## Commercial rule of thumb

- SaaS or hosted deployment: commercial license required
- Embedded or paid product use: commercial license required
- Internal commercial use: commercial license recommended or required depending on agreement terms

## Simple revenue model

A safe public launch can be built around:

1. public MIT repo for visibility and trust
2. commercial license sales for business use
3. optional support and maintenance contracts
4. enterprise pricing for advanced integration and deployment

This is the lowest-friction path to monetization while keeping the public project safe, clean, and commercially defensible.
